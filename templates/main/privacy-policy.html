FLEX MMC (“FLEX”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how FLEX MMC collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our websites, software applications, mobile applications, digital services, and related products.

By using our services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the service you use, we may collect the following information:

Information you provide directly

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Company name and position
  • Account and login information
  • Information submitted through contact forms
  • Customer support requests and messages
  • Other information you voluntarily provide to us

Technical information

When you use our websites or applications, certain technical information may be collected automatically, including:

  • IP address
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Browser type
  • Application version
  • Date and time of access
  • Log and diagnostic information
  • Information regarding interaction with our services

The information collected depends on the specific product or service you use.

2. How We Use Your Information

FLEX may use collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide and operate our services
  • To create and manage user accounts
  • To process and respond to requests
  • To provide customer and technical support
  • To communicate with customers and users
  • To improve software, apps, and services
  • To maintain security and prevent unauthorized access
  • To detect and prevent fraud or security incidents
  • To process transactions where applicable
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not use personal information for purposes incompatible with the purpose for which it was collected without an appropriate legal basis.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

Depending on the circumstances, FLEX may process personal information based on:

  • Your consent;
  • The performance of a contract or provision of requested services;
  • Compliance with legal obligations;
  • Legitimate business interests, where permitted by applicable law;
  • Other lawful grounds permitted by applicable legislation.

You may withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, subject to applicable legal and contractual limitations.

4. Sharing of Information

FLEX does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

We may share information with trusted third-party service providers when necessary to provide or maintain our services. Such providers may include:

Cloud Hosting IT Infrastructure Payment Processing Messaging Services Analytics & Security Technical Support

If you communicate with FLEX through WhatsApp or another messaging platform, the relevant platform may process certain information according to its own privacy policy and terms of service.

FLEX may receive and process information that you voluntarily provide through such communication channels, including:

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill requested services, maintain business records, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

10. International Data Transfers

Depending on third-party service providers used, personal information may be stored or processed on servers located outside the user's country. FLEX takes reasonable measures to ensure processing complies with data protection requirements.
